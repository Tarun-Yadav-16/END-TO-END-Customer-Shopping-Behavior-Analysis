Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis |

Project Overview

This end-to-end data analysis project examines the shopping behavior of 3,900 customers. MySQL was used to answer business questions, while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard that highlights customer demographics, purchasing patterns, product performance, subscription behavior, and revenue trends.

Dashboard Preview



Business Objectives

Analyze revenue generated by male and female customers.

Compare subscribers and non-subscribers by total and average spending.

Identify top-performing products and categories.

Understand the impact of discounts on customer purchases.

Segment customers into New, Returning, and Loyal groups.

Analyze revenue by age group and season.

Compare Standard and Express shipping behavior.

Examine whether repeat buyers are more likely to subscribe.

Key Insights

Clothing generated the highest category revenue at $104,264.

Accessories was the second-highest category with $74,200 in revenue.

Middle-aged customers contributed the highest revenue at $100,829.

Fall recorded the highest seasonal revenue at approximately $60,000.

Only around 27% of customers were subscribed, indicating an opportunity to improve subscription conversion.

SQL Analysis

The project includes 10 business-focused SQL queries covering:

Aggregate functions: SUM(), AVG(), and COUNT()

Common Table Expressions (CTEs)

Conditional logic with CASE

Window functions such as ROW_NUMBER()

Customer segmentation

Product ranking

Discount-rate analysis

Revenue and subscription comparisons

The complete queries are available in Customer_Shopping_Behavior_SQL_Queries.pdf.
